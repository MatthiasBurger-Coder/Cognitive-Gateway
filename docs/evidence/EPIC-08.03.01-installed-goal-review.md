## Lifecycle and compatibility decision

The durable read acquisition uses the existing **Dispatching + Reserved** snapshot state with a typed **Preparation** resource phase. A committed goal manifest transitions to **Runnable + Prepared**; verified completion is **Completed + Verified**. Acquisition and recovery invocation fences are distinct: a recovery may advance the fence, while retaining the immutable historical acquisition binding. An interrupted connector Dispatching claim cannot allocate or read again. Existing ArtifactGoal Dispatching behavior remains on its original branch.

The proposed new public Preparing state was rejected during Three Amigos review because [the session contract](../shared-session-contract.md) freezes executable v2 artifacts. It is absent from the final model. `schemas/codex/v2/common.schema.json` and `response.schema.json` have **zero diff** against the candidate base; the contract regression accepts existing dispatching/reserved and rejects preparing. No lossy projection alias or schema relaxation was used.
